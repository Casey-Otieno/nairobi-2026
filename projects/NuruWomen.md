# Nuru Women

**Hack4Freedom Nairobi 2026**

---

## Overview

Nuru Women is an open-source women’s health knowledge and community platform designed to make reliable health information easier to access, understand, and discuss, particularly for women in Kenya and across Africa.

The platform combines clinically reviewed health information, AI-assisted guidance, privacy-focused tools, research evidence, and community knowledge. It currently focuses on five major areas of women’s health:

- Menstrual health
- Postpartum health
- Sexual and reproductive health
- Mental health
- Healthy ageing

Nuru Women supports English and Kiswahili and is designed to grow into additional languages and regions.

---

## Problem

Women often struggle to access reliable, understandable, and locally relevant health information.

Common challenges include:

- Health misinformation online
- Limited access to clinicians and specialists
- Stigma around reproductive and sexual health
- Lack of locally relevant African health information
- Language barriers
- Privacy concerns when asking sensitive health questions
- Research and knowledge gaps affecting women’s health

Many existing platforms also depend on centralized systems where users have limited control over their identity, data, and content.

---

## Solution

Nuru Women provides a privacy-conscious platform where users can explore health information, ask questions, review research evidence, and participate in community discussions.

The platform includes:

- AI-assisted health information analysis
- Privacy checks before sensitive information is submitted
- Safety checks for potentially harmful or urgent health situations
- English and Kiswahili support
- Clinically reviewed research and evidence
- Research-gap and health-data visualizations
- Topic-based health libraries
- Community questions and answers
- Nostr-based decentralized communication and identity capabilities
- An architecture designed for future Bitcoin and Lightning integration

The platform does not replace professional medical care. Its purpose is to improve access to trustworthy information and help users make better-informed decisions about seeking appropriate healthcare.
